How this model works
  • One step is one day, and everyone updates at the same moment.
  • 400 people live on a map. Each infectious person meets the set number of people nearby each day.
  • 20 hubs make 5 times as many contacts.
  • Recovered people stay immune for the whole run.
  • From their second infectious day, people may isolate and then make no more contacts.
  • Vaccinated people cannot catch it. Real vaccines are not perfect, so this is a simplification.
Seabrook and the Zephyr virus are fictional. This is a model, not a medical prediction.
