SEABROOK AFTER DARK

In this game world the Zephyr virus has turned people into zombies. Pick a hero, cure the zombies, and protect the survivors.

The twist: every hero's special power changes one variable from the Outbreak Lab model. After each wave, the computer runs the same outbreak with no hero so you can compare.

This is a game, not a medical prediction. Zombies here are cured, not hurt: they turn back into ordinary people.
